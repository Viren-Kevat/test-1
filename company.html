( 01 )   Overview

Technology that quietly carries the world.

Nexora began as three engineers and a single idea. It is now a company of 62,000 people serving more than 3,000 of the world’s largest organisations across banking, healthcare, government, energy and retail.

We design, build and run the platforms behind critical services — from payments that settle in milliseconds to hospital systems that cannot go down. Our work is largely invisible by design: when it is done well, nobody notices.

( 02 )   What we do

Four pillars,
one promise.

Cloud & Infrastructure

Sovereign, resilient and carbon-aware cloud for regulated industries, with data that stays where the law says it must.

  • Private, hybrid and sovereign cloud regions
  • Managed infrastructure and site reliability
  • Carbon-aware workload scheduling

AI & Data

Applied intelligence that turns enterprise data into confident decisions, with explainability and human oversight built in.

  • Aether platform for models and agents
  • Data engineering and analytics at scale
  • An independent AI ethics board

Cybersecurity

Zero-trust defence, monitored around the clock from twelve global centres staffed by more than 4,000 specialists.

  • Managed detection and response
  • Identity, access and zero-trust architecture
  • Incident readiness and recovery

Digital Transformation

End-to-end modernisation for organisations reinventing how they operate, from legacy core systems to customer experience.

  • Application and core-system modernisation
  • Product engineering and design
  • Change management and reskilling
