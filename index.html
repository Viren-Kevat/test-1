( 01 )   About

An engineer at heart, a builder by conviction.

Viren Kevat began a career writing code for a small start-up. In 2002 came the founding of Nexora Technologies, from a rented garage with three engineers and a single idea: enterprise technology should feel effortless.

Today Nexora serves more than 3,000 of the world’s largest organisations across 45 countries. The guiding belief has never changed — technology only matters when it improves people’s lives — and it shapes every product, partnership and decision at the company.

Six beliefs that

01

Think in decades

Quarterly results matter. Lasting trust matters more. We build for the long horizon.
02

Stay curious

The best answer is rarely the first one. Ask better questions, then ask again.
03

Respect craft

( 03 )   Journey

Three decades,
one long horizon.

From a junior engineer’s first commit to a group of six companies — five chapters that shaped everything since.

1994

The first line of code

Joins a Boston software start-up as a junior engineer, learning how great products are built — and how easily they fail.

2002

Nexora is founded

Starts the company from a rented garage with three engineers, delivering its first enterprise platform within eleven months.

2011

Going global

Nexora lists publicly and opens delivery centres across Europe and Asia, growing from 400 to 12,000 people in five years.

2018

Nexora Cloud launches

A sovereign, carbon-aware cloud platform becomes the fastest-growing business in the company’s history.

2024

An AI-first decade

Commits $1 billion to responsible AI research and reskilling 100,000 professionals by 2030.
